Message from the representative

Basics, done well.

If there is one thing we do well, it is the basics.

We train every member of staff to work to the same standard and to share the company's mission, vision and values, and that is how we reach for a higher level of service. When everyone aims at the same goal, a team finds its rhythm, momentum and harmony. Good coordination makes the work faster and the mistakes fewer, and that is what guests feel.

Seirai Group Japan Ltd.ASAWA SIRIKAN, Representative Director

Who we are

Philosophy, vision and mission.

What we promise, where we are going, and what we provide.

  1. 01Philosophy

    Keeping the promise is how we earn trust.

    We promise a high standard, and keep that promise in every part of the business we build. That is how we earn the trust of everyone we serve.

  2. 02Vision

    To become Japan's most beloved family hospitality brand.

    A family-first, private, home-like alternative: no lobby, no fuss, just comfort, convenience and privacy.

  3. 03Mission

    Provide space for families to make lasting memories together.

    Private, clean and comfortable accommodation, with friendly service and personal touches, and the latest technology to make each stay more comfortable and convenient.

Management philosophy

The principles behind the promise.

The principles we hold to, so that the promise is made and kept.

  1. 01Trust builds the brand

    Marketing does not build a brand; trust does. Reviews, referrals, returning guests and growing partnerships all follow from trust earned one promise at a time.

  2. 02Standard is our greatest asset

    We work to a high standard so that the surprises guests find with us are good ones.

  3. 03Integrity before profit

    With guests, staff, partners and shareholders, we keep our word. When we fall short, we are honest about it and do what is right to fix it.

  4. 04Reward outcomes, fund growth

    We reward outcomes, and we fund the growth that makes them possible, by investing in the people who deliver them.

  5. 05Excellence creates value

    When we operate at the highest standard, guests, employees, owners and neighbourhoods all benefit.

  6. 06Hold the standard, and pride follows

    Standard → promise → trust → integrity → outcomes → value → pride. Pride is the proof of work done right, not the reason for doing it.

Always aim for improvement

Quality rests on the system, not on any one person.

Each clean is checked before it is closed, each problem is weighed against our SOP, and each issue is tracked until it is resolved. Every record then feeds back into how we work and how we train, so the standard keeps improving, whoever is on shift.

  1. 01A check before the clean is closed

    Whoever cleaned a room cannot close the clean until the room has been checked against its checklist.

  2. 02An inspection by management

    Management also inspects rooms, and no inspector grades their own clean. Each problem is weighed by AI against our SOP.

  3. 03Issues, repairs and restocking, tracked

    Issues, maintenance and restocking are all logged in our system and tracked until they are resolved, so that nothing is missed and every room stays well maintained.

  4. 04Every record, analysed

    Every inspection is kept on record and analysed as it comes in, and what it shows is turned into solutions in real time, so that the same mistake is not repeated and the way we work improves every day.

  5. 05Our in-house training course

    New staff learn through our own in-house training course: an intensive programme that covers the work thoroughly, and the mindset behind it.

Training

The same care, whoever is on shift.

Our training exists so that everyone on the team works the same way and shares the company's philosophy, vision and mission. What a guest finds should not depend on who is on shift, and should hold as the company grows. The training covers safety, skill and mindset.

  1. 01Safety

    Safety before anything else.

    The first thing staff learn is to work safely: how to handle cleaning products, work at height, lift, and work alone. Nothing we do is worth an injury.

  2. 02Skill

    The work, learned on the job.

    The work itself is learned at real properties: cleaning in a set order, leaving each room arranged and not only clean, and checking it area by area.

  3. 03Mindset

    Knowing why, not only how.

    Staff learn our six values, the shared basis for how we think and decide. They study the company's vision and mission, so that they understand what the work is for. The aim is that each decision they make on shift upholds our philosophy: keeping the promise is how we earn trust.
