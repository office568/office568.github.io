Careers

Careers at Seirai Group Japan Ltd., which runs ACOE accommodation in Tokyo for families and groups: what each role involves and how to apply.

  1. 01The work
  2. 02The roles
  3. 03How to apply

The work

Looking after stays for families and groups in Tokyo.

We run ACOE, accommodation in Tokyo for families and groups who want more than a place to sleep. Together, as a team, we look after our guests, the cleaning and inspection of each room, and the upkeep of the buildings.

The work is learned at real properties, paired with an experienced colleague who guides you. [TO CONFIRM: that this applies to every role] It starts simple and builds up step by step. The training covers safety, skill and mindset.

The roles

Three roles.

  1. 01Residence Manager

    Looks after properties and guests, day to day.

    You look after the day-to-day running of your assigned properties. You handle guest messages, and when something happens on site, you deal with it and decide what to do. Cleaning, room inspection, inventory checks, and on-site maintenance and repair are done together with your teammates. Hiring status [TO CONFIRM] / Employment type [TO CONFIRM] / Japanese language requirement [TO CONFIRM]

  2. 02General Manager

    Leads the residence managers and trains new staff.

    You lead the residence managers day to day and carry the company's direction, vision and mission to the team. You train new staff, teaching the purpose behind each task as well as how to do it. You handle guest issues that need a decision, oversee maintenance and repair, and join in with cleaning and room inspection on site. Hiring status [TO CONFIRM] / Employment type [TO CONFIRM] / Japanese language requirement [TO CONFIRM]

  3. 03Part-time staff [TO CONFIRM]

    Preparing rooms, on site.

    The work centres on room cleaning, building upkeep and room inspection. Rooms are cleaned in a set order and checked area by area. When something comes up on site, you resolve it in contact with the team, and the work is done together with your teammates. Hiring status [TO CONFIRM] / Employment type [TO CONFIRM] / Japanese language requirement [TO CONFIRM]

How to apply

Apply by email.

Please email your CV [TO CONFIRM] and a short note to office@seiraigroup.com, with the name of the role in the subject line (for example, "Application: Residence Manager").

[TO CONFIRM: how applicants are contacted] We will read your application and contact the applicants we would like to meet.

[TO CONFIRM] Working conditions are set out for each applicant before any interview.

Application documents are used only to consider your application and are not returned. For details, please see our Privacy Policy.
